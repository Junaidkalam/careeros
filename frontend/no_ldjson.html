Senior Backend Engineer

Company: Acme Cloud Inc.

Location: Austin, TX (Hybrid)

Employment Type: Full-Time

Date Posted: 2024-06-01

Base Salary: $140,000 - $180,000 per year

About the Role

Acme Cloud is looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Platform Infrastructure team. You will design and own distributed systems powering millions of customers worldwide. This is a high-impact role — you will lead technical initiatives, mentor junior engineers, and collaborate cross-functionally with product and design.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable microservices using Java and Spring Boot.
  • Own and evolve core platform APIs consumed by dozens of internal teams.
  • Drive reliability improvements: SLOs, on-call, incident response, and postmortems.
  • Mentor 2-3 junior engineers through code review and pairing sessions.
  • Collaborate with product managers to scope and deliver quarterly features.

Required Skills

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
  • Deep expertise in Java or Kotlin
  • Strong experience with Spring Boot and REST API design
  • Experience with PostgreSQL or other relational databases
  • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and Docker
  • Familiarity with AWS or GCP cloud infrastructure

Preferred Skills

  • Experience with Kafka or other event-streaming platforms
  • Familiarity with gRPC and Protocol Buffers
  • Prior experience in a startup or high-growth environment
  • Contributions to open-source projects

About Acme Cloud

Acme Cloud Inc. is a B2B SaaS company headquartered in Austin, TX, delivering cloud infrastructure tooling to over 5,000 enterprise clients globally. Founded in 2018, we have grown to 400+ employees and are Series C funded.
